TASK INSTRUCTIONS

In this study you will be asked to perform a series of tasks that involve performing different number of steps.

When prompted, start performing the steps with the step simulator under your desk. When a given number of steps is reached, you will proceed to the next task. Try performing the steps as quickly as you can.

After each task, use the scales presented on the screen to rate the different characteristics of the task that you just performed. Use the [F] and [K] keys to navigate the scale and confirm your choice by pressing [SPACE]. There will be a 15 second rest period following each task.
